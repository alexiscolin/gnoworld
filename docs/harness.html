How the AI builds gnoworld

One slice at a time. Alexis decides; a chief session plans and integrates; each branch runs in a session and a worktree of its own, as a loop the gate checks at every commit. Nothing reaches main without the full gate, a recorded review and Alexis's merge.

The gate, at every step

  • Every edit and command: guard.py and the sandbox (no hook skipped, no secret read, no harness edit in main)
  • Every turn: the Stop hook wants the fast gate green on exactly this tree
  • Every commit: the fast gate (five lanes, 30 s scoped, 120 s for everything), the secret scan
  • Every merge: the full gate's lanes: coverage, mutations, regressions, the harness proving itself
  • Every push of main: the full gate's record and the review pass, for exactly that tree

Agents

  • Fresh, briefed from files, never forks; four at most at once
  • Read-only: they report one row per finding, with its evidence
  • A finding is a claim until a command or a failing test shows it
  • Every finding goes to the ledger: fixed, rejected, intended, moot or asked
  • Tokens and hours per branch measured, against slice 4's

Memory lives in files

  • docs/plans/: what to build, and when it is done
  • docs/decisions.md: every decision of Alexis's, with its reason
  • docs/review/findings.md: every finding, with its evidence
  • A state file per branch, one shared learnings file
  • CLAUDE.md and .claude/: the rules, which the dream proposes to change
