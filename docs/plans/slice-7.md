## 4. Cost
Per frame, bounded by the distance ladder, never by the universe (ADR-0002 §3): planets, dust and moons instanced, static meshes merged per material, labels pooled and moved, their text changed 10 times a second at most. Bench (CPU slowed 4×) at 100, 1,000 and 10,000 realms: draw calls and frame time flat past the cap, with the draw bound recorded as a budget. Placement and planet parameters O(1) per realm, in a worker. Weight, measured today with esbuild 0.28.2, a bare scene, minified, gzip -9: three 0.187.0's WebGL renderer 133,691 B; its `three/webgpu` entry (the renderer with its WebGL2 fallback, ADR-0001 §12) 221,519 B. The first screen's cap is 173 KiB (177,152 B) and 7a must not move it, so three is a lazy chunk. The Earth's two textures at 4096 × 2048, WebP, take about 89 MB of GPU memory with their mipmaps, computed (4 B a pixel, a third more), not measured; the Low tier halves each side. The gate's price: `ALWAYS` in `scripts/harness_mutate.py` keys every verdict on `pnpm-lock.yaml`, so an install voids all 1,141: the next `--full` judges them all, about 6 h at the 3 a minute measured on 2026-11-01. So every dependency of this slice goes in at once, in one install.
